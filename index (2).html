About me

I'm a Mobile/Android Device Test Engineer with over three years of experience in performance, power consumption and thermal testing for Android devices. I'm comfortable with the Android Framework and mobile hardware architecture, and I use dedicated equipment such as Power Monsoon, Overcurrent Meter, Thermal Imager, Thermocouple and High-Speed Camera to validate devices at hardware level.

I build automated test frameworks with Python, Selenium, Pytest, Playwright and Appium, and I'm exploring how AI agents can speed up log triage and anomaly detection. I work with global cross-functional teams, so clear communication is part of everything I do.
